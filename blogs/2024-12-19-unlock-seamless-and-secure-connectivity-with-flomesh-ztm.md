<p>Embrace the future of secure, seamless connectivity with Flomesh ZTM.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/proxy/1*J4zp7X6xzFjYA8e8DwR_9A.png" /></figure><p>In today’s interconnected world, ensuring secure and efficient communication across diverse networks is paramount. Flomesh’s Zero Trust Mesh (ZTM) emerges as a cutting-edge, open-source solution designed to provide seamless connectivity while adhering to zero-trust security principles.</p><h4>What Is Flomesh ZTM?</h4><p><a href="https://github.com/flomesh-io/ztm">Flomesh ZTM</a> is a privacy-first, open-source decentralized network software that facilitates secure communication across various network environments, including LANs, containerized networks, and the Internet. By leveraging HTTP/2 tunnels, ZTM ensures fast, encrypted, and reliable connections between services and devices.</p><h4>Key Features of Flomesh ZTM</h4><ol><li><strong>Fast and Efficient Communication</strong></li><li>ZTM utilizes HTTP/2 multiplexing, allowing multiple data streams over a single connection, enhancing transmission efficiency and reducing latency. Built on Pipy, a high-performance programmable proxy, ZTM delivers exceptional speed and responsiveness.</li><li><strong>Secure TLS-Encrypted Channels</strong></li><li>All traffic within ZTM is encrypted using TLS, ensuring data confidentiality and integrity. Certificate-based authentication further verifies the identity of communicating parties, establishing a trusted environment for data exchange.</li><li><strong>Highly Customizable and Programmable</strong></li><li>Leveraging Flomesh <a href="https://github.com/flomesh-io/pipy">Pipy</a>’s scripting capabilities, ZTM offers extensive customization, allowing users to tailor network behavior, implement custom security policies, and adapt protocols to meet specific organizational requirements.</li><li><strong>Portability Across Platforms</strong></li><li>ZTM is designed for versatility, running on various CPU architectures (x86, ARM, MIPS, RISC-V, LoongArch) and operating systems (Linux, Windows, macOS, FreeBSD, Android). This cross-platform compatibility ensures flexibility in diverse deployment scenarios.</li><li><strong>Decentralized Application Publishing and Deployment</strong></li><li>ZTM supports decentralized application publishing, enabling services to be distributed across the network without relying on centralized servers, enhancing resilience and reducing single points of failure.</li><li><strong>Decentralized File Discovery and Data Sharing</strong></li><li>Facilitating decentralized file discovery, ZTM allows users to share documents, pictures, and videos within groups, ensuring privacy and control over shared content without dependence on traditional social networking platforms.</li></ol><h4>Real-World Use Case: Remote Access to Home Networks</h4><p>Imagine securely accessing your home computer or NAS device from anywhere in the world without the need for a fixed public IP address. ZTM enables seamless remote access, allowing you to retrieve important files, manage devices, and maintain connectivity with your home network, regardless of your location.</p><h4>Getting Started with Flomesh ZTM</h4><ul><li><strong>Explore the ZTM Wiki</strong></li><li>For detailed information and diverse use-case scenarios, visit the <a href="https://github.com/flomesh-io/ztm/wiki">ZTM Wiki</a>.</li><li><strong>Deploy ZTM Hub via AWS AMI</strong></li><li>ZTM HomeConnect is available as an Amazon Machine Image (AMI) on AWS Marketplace. This offering enables you to securely connect to your personal files, media, and devices without geographical or network restrictions. Learn more and deploy via <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-2piugxrunh3gi?applicationId=AWSMPContessa">AWS Marketplace</a>.</li><li><strong>Access Pre-Compiled ZTM Agents</strong></li><li>Pre-compiled ZTM agents are available for various platforms, including Windows, macOS (x86/aarch64), Linux (x86/arm64), and Android. Download them from the <a href="https://github.com/flomesh-io/ztm/releases">GitHub Releases page</a>. An iOS app is also available via the Apple <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ztm/id6584517193">App Store</a>.</li></ul><h4>Summary</h4><p>Flomesh ZTM revolutionizes secure connectivity by providing a fast, customizable, and decentralized network solution. Its cross-platform compatibility and robust security features make it an ideal choice for individuals and organizations seeking to enhance their network communication while maintaining strict privacy controls.</p><img alt="" height="1" src="https://medium.com/_/stat?event=post.clientViewed&amp;referrerSource=full_rss&amp;postId=8513b5c4cbb6" width="1" /><hr /><p><a href="https://blog.flomesh.io/unlock-seamless-and-secure-connectivity-with-flomesh-ztm-8513b5c4cbb6">Unlock Seamless and Secure Connectivity with Flomesh ZTM</a> was originally published in <a href="https://blog.flomesh.io">Flomesh</a> on Medium, where people are continuing the conversation by highlighting and responding to this story.</p>
